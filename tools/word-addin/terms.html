2. Service description

The Add-in provides access to a database of Swiss court decisions and statutes directly within Microsoft Word. Users can search decisions and statute articles and insert formatted citations. With a Pro subscription, users can additionally look up related decisions in the citation graph, verify references and find supporting decisions using AI-assisted analysis, enrich a paragraph with leading cases, check every citation in a document, and obtain a literary reflection on a draft.

3. Tiers and pricing

You are solely responsible for verifying the accuracy of any information before relying on it in legal proceedings or advice.

Redaction of personal data. Before any Pro request is sent, the Add-in replaces certain categories of personal data in the submitted text with placeholders: e-mail addresses, Swiss AHV/AVS numbers, Swiss IBANs, Swiss company identification numbers (CHE), Swiss phone numbers, dates of birth introduced by a birth formula, street addresses, postal codes with place names, and personal names preceded by a title. This step is pattern-based and best-effort. It does not detect all personal data; in particular, names without a preceding title, dates without a birth formula and non-Swiss identifiers are transmitted as written. Its scope and limits are described in the Privacy Policy. We give no warranty that any particular personal data will be removed.
