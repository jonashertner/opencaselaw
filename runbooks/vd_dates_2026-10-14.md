# Runbook: Vaud Tribunal cantonal dates from the ruling's own heading (2026-10-14)

**What is wrong (report 2026-10-13, A5).** `vd_findinfo_AI_73_24_-_366_2025___________` is
served on 2021-01-01, an estimate, tagged "de" although its text is French. Audit of the

The heading counts only between the header's underline rules ("COUR D'APPEL PENALE ______
Séance du 23 juillet 2015 ______ Composition"); a date in the facts ("dans une séance du 21
mai 2014", "Par jugement incident du …") never qualifies. There is no bound from the
findinfo number or from publication_date: the number carries the publication year, and
publication can come long after the ruling; publication_date in the entscheidsuche copy
often holds the ruling date itself.

The stored value is mostly the date of the decision under review ("Jug / 2015 / 329"
stored 2015-03-13, "Séance du 23 juillet 2015"). Sampled changes read against the text:
32, all right, including 8 with a gap over a year. Local build of the three VD shards
before/after: 9,336 served dates corrected, 146 languages, placeholders 331 -> 186 (no
heading in the rest), vd_gerichte -0.07 % (44 duplicates folded, aliases recorded),
vd_findinfo and vd_omni unchanged.

**Live scraper (checked 2026-10-14).** `scrapers/cantonal/vd_gerichte.py` stores the
portal's `dateDecision` as is. Of 12,732 rows scraped since 2026-09, 163 (1.3 %) disagree
with their heading: 2 are after their own publication (impossible), 30 are the date the
dispositif was communicated, 29 another dated event of the text (the lower court's ruling,
a filing), 59 a few days off and 43 further off without the text stating them. The first
three classes are the portal's metadata, not our parsing; the last two cannot be judged
from the text and the repair leaves the ambiguous ones alone.

**Both shards go together.** vd_gerichte holds the same rulings with the same wrong dates;
repairing only vd_findinfo splits 33 pairs the cross-court dedup folds today, and the
vd_gerichte copy comes back as a separate row with the wrong date.

**Not in this runbook (needs approval, scraper change).** Until the scraper prefers the
heading date over `dateDecision` in the clear classes above, new rows of that kind keep
arriving (about 1 % of intake).
