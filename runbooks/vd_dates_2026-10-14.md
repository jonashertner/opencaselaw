vd_gerichte, all right. The findinfo number's year is the publication year ("HC / 2018 /
27" for an "Arrêt du 22 décembre 2017"), so a heading one year earlier is accepted.

**Both shards go together.** vd_gerichte holds the same rulings with the same wrong dates;
repairing only vd_findinfo splits 33 pairs the cross-court dedup folds today, and the
vd_gerichte copy comes back as a separate row with the wrong date.

**Not in this runbook (needs approval, scraper change).** 119 of the vd_gerichte rows
were scraped in 2026-09 by the live scraper: the portal's `dateDecision` itself differs from
the heading for a subset. Until `scrapers/cantonal/vd_gerichte.py` prefers the heading date
over `dateDecision` when they disagree, new rows of that kind keep arriving.
