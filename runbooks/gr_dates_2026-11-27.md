## 1. GR rulings dated by the decision under appeal

**What is wrong.** GR PZ 2006 225 is served as 14 November 2006, the date of the
Kreispräsident's ruling it reviews; its letterhead reads "Chur, 16. April 2007". A GR
ruling states its own date in its head, in one of two layouts:

* Kantonsgericht until ~2019: the letterhead `Ref.: Chur, 16. April 2007` (Italian
  `Rif.: Coira, 21 agosto 2018`), then "Schriftlich mitgeteilt am", the docket, the bench,
  and "Im Rekurs … gegen die Verfügung des Kreispräsidenten Chur vom 14. November 2006".
* Kantonsgericht since, Verwaltungsgericht, Obergericht: `Entscheid vom 02. Mai 2022`,
  `URTEIL vom 19. Juni 2018`, `SENTENZA del 31 ottobre 2022`, `Decisione dell'8 aprile 2026`.

Two passes put another date there:

* `scripts/repair_source_dates.py` (2026-11-08), rule `gr_head`: `head_vom_date` takes the
  first "<kind> … vom <date>" in the first 2,500 characters. In the older layout there is no
  "vom" in the letterhead, so it read "Verfügung des Kreispräsidenten Chur vom …", the
  decision under appeal, or a Federal Court ruling noted under the docket ("(Eine gegen
  diese Entscheidung erhobene Beschwerde hat das Bundesgericht mit Urteil vom … abgewiesen.)").
  It was allowed to correct rows scraped before the Tribuna fix and rewrote 1,479 direct and
  521 es rows; 1,286 es copies followed by `twin_follow`. PZ 2006 225 went from 2007-04-16
  (right) to 2006-11-14 on 11-08.
* `scripts/repair_decision_dates.py --all` (2026-03-12) did the same to rows the 11-08
  rule then "confirmed" (the same wrong date): 716 direct rows carry its `date_extraction`
  stamp and get their first repair now.

**The fix.**

* `source_dates.gr_head_date`: the letterhead "Chur/Cuira/Coira, <date>" if there is one,
  else "Entscheid/Urteil/Beschluss/Verfügung/Sentenza/Decisione/… vom/del/dell'/dals <date>";
  only in the head, which ends at the parties, the decision under appeal ("Im Rekurs",
  "gegen die", "Anfechtungsobj."), the bench/parties/subject blocks of the newer layouts,
  the notification line, or a noted Federal Court ruling ("(Mit Urteil", "(Eine gegen").
* `repair_source_dates.py`, gr_gerichte: rule `gr_own_head` (CORRECT) ahead of the old
  `gr_head` (now the fallback when the head states no date). Like `gr_head`, it never
  second-guesses a row scraped after the Tribuna fix (2026-09-28: the portal field is the
  court's own); it may also correct a row whose date the 03-12 pass wrote.
* `repair_decision_dates.py`: `gr_gerichte` and `es_gr_gerichte` in `NEVER_REPAIR_SHARDS`.
* Tests: `tests/test_gr_head_dates_2026_11_27.py` (8, mutation-checked).

## 2. OCR-damaged Erwägung headings in old BGE volumes

**What is wrong.** BGE 55 II 235: the OCR read the heading of E. 4 as "4 .. -".
`ERW_PARA_RE` takes one dot and one dash, so E. 4 ran on inside E. 3 (`siblings` 2, 3;
`cite` called E. 4 invalid). HF-mirror count 2026-11-27 with the real extractor: 119 BGE
rulings, about 120 Erwägungen, volumes 20–79 plus three later ones ("2, -", "3: -",
"4.. Die", "6 . .-: Auf"). A floor: markers whose digit itself is misread are not counted.

**The fix.** `search_stack/extract_decision_structure.py`, `_admit_ocr_tops`: after the
sequence is validated, a line holding a 1–2 digit number and at least two punctuation
marks, then nothing or a sentence, is admitted as top-level E. N only where the marker
before it belongs to E. N-1, the next marker (if any) to a higher number, no E. N exists
yet, and the context reads as a heading (`_ocr_top_context_ok`):

* never an amount ("50.--"); a standalone marker must be followed by a sentence;
* a dots-only standalone marker ("5 ..........") is the printed mark of an omitted
  Erwägung when the dispositive or a section follows, or less than 200 characters before
  the next heading (a running head, the printer's line): not admitted;
* outside the historical BGE volumes (where the scan puts running heads and page numbers
  above headings, and lines open with dates), also: the line before ends a sentence and not
  with an abbreviation ("consid.", "Fr.", "E."), and no item N-1 of a quoted list stands
  in the 800 characters above.

Tests: `tests/test_ocr_erwaegung_markers_2026_11_27.py` (6, mutation-checked).

**Regression run** (2026-11-27, HF mirror, old vs new extractor on every ruling of 15
courts, 552,000 rows: bge, bger, bvger, bstger, ti, gr, zh_obergericht,
zh_verwaltungsgericht, fr, be_verwaltungsgericht, bl, bs_appellationsgericht, ne,
sg_versicherungsgericht, vs). The first draft (sequence rule only) changed 18 non-BGE
rulings, 5–6 of them wrongly (a citation and two amounts broken across lines, two quoted
lists, a date); the context rules above came from those. Final: 105 BGE rulings change
(99 of the 119 counted, plus 6 real headings the count had missed) and 10 others, all real
headings: BGer 1A.282/1999 E. 51–52 (a ruling with more than 50 Erwägungen),
H 302/98 E. 5, P 15/99 E. 4, C 251/01 E. 2 (each under two ids), ZH PQ190008 E. 10,
SG IV 2010/387 E. 6, VS FI 19 123 E. 5. No ruling loses a top-level Erwägung; lettered
units move to their real parent (BGE 74 I 120: "2a–2c" become "3a–3c"). A sample of 15 new
BGE Erwägungen all open where the ruling's reasoning step opens. Not fixed: 20 of the 119
(an E. 1 with no E. 0 before it, markers followed by OCR debris the rules cannot tell from
a running head).

**Deploy.** Nothing to run: the extractor's source hash changes, so the build after the
ff-merge re-extracts every decision in step 2g (≈ 4 h more than an incremental 2g, which
