01  Inside the corpus

An open dataset of Swiss law.

1’077’024 decisions
Published rulings from the Federal Supreme Court, every federal court, and all 26 cantons, 1875–2026, in German, French and Italian.
9’622 ECtHR judgments
The Swiss-respondent case law, plus every Chamber and Grand Chamber judgment of importance 1–3 against any of the 46 Council of Europe states, 1960–2026.
21’153 laws
5,525 federal laws (Fedlex) and 15,600 cantonal acts, with 494,000+ articles.
