# Sernfeld Group AI Policy (version 1.0, approved by the Executive Board on 4 December 2023)

1. Every AI use case has a named owner and is entered in the AI register.
2. High-risk use cases (personnel decisions, safety functions, medical devices) need board approval.
3. Personal data only after a data protection impact assessment.
4. Outputs that affect people are reviewed by a person.
5. The register is reviewed annually by the Audit Committee.
