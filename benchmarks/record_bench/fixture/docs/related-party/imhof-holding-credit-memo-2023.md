# Credit memo — loan to Imhof Beteiligungen AG (August 2023)

Prepared by: Beat Hug (Group Treasurer).
Borrower: Imhof Beteiligungen AG; assets: 52 % of Sernfeld Antriebstechnik AG (value approx. CHF 260 m), real estate CHF 14 m; debt CHF 9 m.
Loan: CHF 12.0 m, 3 years, 3.25 % interest, bullet repayment 30 September 2026; security: pledge of 600,000 Sernfeld shares (loan-to-value 40 %).
Arm's length: interest rate above the ESTV safe-harbour rate for CHF loans to shareholders for 2023; comparable bank offer 3.10 %.
Repayment capacity: dividends received from Sernfeld (approx. CHF 4.4 m p.a.) cover interest; repayment by refinancing or sale of real estate.
