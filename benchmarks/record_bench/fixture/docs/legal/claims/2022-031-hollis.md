# Claim file 2022-031 — Hollis et al. v. Sernfeld Drives Inc.

Court: U.S. District Court (fictional docket 3:22-cv-00871), product-liability action. Filed 14 September 2022.
Plaintiffs: four warehouse workers injured when a hydraulic lift platform fitted with a Sernfeld HX-40 actuator drive descended uncontrolled (Gastonia, NC, 2 March 2022).
Claimed damages: USD 60 million (compensatory and punitive).
Defence counsel: outside counsel in Charlotte, NC. Coverage: product-liability insurance USD 20 million, reservation of rights.
Parent guarantee: Sernfeld Antriebstechnik AG guarantee of 3 May 2019 for the obligations of Sernfeld Drives Inc. up to USD 75 million.
Provision in the parent's stand-alone accounts: CHF 22.0 million (FY2022, FY2023); CHF 34.0 million (FY2024); CHF 40.0 million (FY2025).
