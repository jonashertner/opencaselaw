# Product release decision — SX-Ex explosion-protected pump drive

Product Release Committee (CEO, Head of Regulatory Affairs and Quality, Head of Sales), 22 October 2020.

Product: SX-Ex 7.5–55 kW pump drives for use in potentially explosive atmospheres (gas group IIB, temperature class T4), equipment group II, category 2G.
Conformity: EU-type examination certificate by ExCert Prüfstelle GmbH (fictional notified body), docs/products/sx-ex/ex-certificate-2020.md; declaration of conformity docs/products/sx-ex/declaration-of-conformity-2020.md (Swiss rules on equipment for explosive atmospheres; ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU).
Decision: release for sale from 1 November 2020 in Switzerland, the EU and the EEA (Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein) only. Sales to any other market need a new release (local certification such as IECEx or national schemes).
Restriction: not for zone 0 / category 1G applications.
Owner: Head of Regulatory Affairs and Quality. Fallback: CEO. Review: 31 October 2022 (certificate surveillance audit).
