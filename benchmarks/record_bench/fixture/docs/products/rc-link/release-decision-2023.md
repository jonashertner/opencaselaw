# Product release decision — RC-Link radio drive controller

Product Release Committee, 19 October 2023.

Product: RC-Link, a drive controller with a Bluetooth Low Energy module (2.4 GHz) for commissioning by smartphone app.
Conformity: declaration of conformity under the Swiss telecommunications installations ordinance and the Radio Equipment Directive 2014/53/EU (docs/products/rc-link/declaration-of-conformity-2023.md); UK: UKCA declaration under the Radio Equipment Regulations 2017.
Decision: release for Switzerland, the EU/EEA and the United Kingdom from 1 November 2023. Restriction: the app may not be offered in other countries' app stores until the radio module is approved there.
Owner: Head of Regulatory Affairs and Quality. Fallback: CEO. Review: 31 October 2025.
