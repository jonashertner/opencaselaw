# Declaration of conformity — RC-Link

Sernfeld Antriebstechnik AG declares that the RC-Link drive controller with radio module BLE-24 meets the essential requirements for radio equipment (health and safety, electromagnetic compatibility, efficient use of the radio spectrum). Standards: EN 300 328 V2.2.2, EN 301 489-17, EN 62368-1. Winterthur, 19 October 2023. Signed: Dr. Nadia Rüegg.
