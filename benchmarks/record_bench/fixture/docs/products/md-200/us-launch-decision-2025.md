# Product release decision — MD-200 in the United States

Joint decision of the Product Release Committee of Sernfeld Antriebstechnik AG and the board of Sernfeld Drives Inc., 26 June 2025.

Basis: FDA establishment registration and device listing of Sernfeld Drives Inc. as initial importer; classification of the MD-200 as a class I device exempt from premarket notification (product code and regulation per US counsel memo of 2 June 2025, docs/products/md-200/us-regulatory-memo-2025.md).
Decision: sale in the United States only, by Sernfeld Drives Inc., from 1 August 2025. Not in Canada or Mexico.
Swiss side: export from Switzerland to Sernfeld Drives Inc. Swiss medical-device rules on the export of devices remain applicable to the Swiss manufacturer.
Owner: Jordan Pryce (Associate General Counsel, Sernfeld Drives Inc.). Fallback: Matteo Bianchi. Review: 30 June 2026.
