# Product release decision — MD-200 medical drive unit

Product Release Committee, 20 May 2021.

Product: MD-200 linear drive and control box for patient lifts and care beds; medical device class I (intended purpose: drive component placed on the market as an accessory to patient lifts).
Conformity: declaration of conformity docs/products/md-200/declaration-of-conformity-2021.md; technical documentation TD-MD200-v3.
Regulatory note (RA): The Swiss medical devices ordinance is being replaced on 26 May 2021; from that date the mutual recognition with the EU for medical devices lapses. For the EU market an EU authorised representative is required; Sernfeld appoints MedRep Europe B.V. (fictional, Rotterdam). For Switzerland the manufacturer is Swiss and registers itself.
Decision: release for sale in Switzerland and the EU/EEA from 1 June 2021. No sales to other markets (in particular not the USA, the UK or Brazil) without a new release.
Owner: Head of Regulatory Affairs and Quality. Fallback: CEO. Review: 31 May 2022.
