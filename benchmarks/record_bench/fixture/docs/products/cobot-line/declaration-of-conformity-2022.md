# Declaration of conformity — CoBot-Line

Sernfeld Antriebstechnik AG declares that the CoBot-Line collaborative handling cell conforms to the Swiss machinery ordinance and Directive 2006/42/EC; standards EN ISO 10218-2:2011, ISO/TS 15066:2016. Winterthur, 15 September 2022. Signed: Dr. Nadia Rüegg.
