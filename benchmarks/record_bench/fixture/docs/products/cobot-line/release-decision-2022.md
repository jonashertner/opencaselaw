# Product release decision — CoBot-Line collaborative handling cell

Product Release Committee, 15 September 2022.

Product: CoBot-Line, a collaborative handling cell (robot arm with Sernfeld drives, safety-rated speed and separation monitoring) sold as a complete machine.
Conformity: risk assessment RA-CBL-2022-07; declaration of conformity under the Swiss machinery ordinance and Directive 2006/42/EC (docs/products/cobot-line/declaration-of-conformity-2022.md).
Product liability: the design review recorded that a product is defective if it does not offer the safety a person is entitled to expect; the instructions therefore warn against use without the safety scanner.
Decision: release for Switzerland and the EU/EEA from 1 October 2022. Restriction: only with the integrated safety scanner; no sale of the arm without the cell.
Owner: Head of Regulatory Affairs and Quality. Fallback: CEO. Review: 30 September 2024.
