# CHF 250,000,000 Syndicated Facilities Agreement (extract)

Dated 28 November 2019 between Sernfeld Antriebstechnik AG as borrower and guarantor, the subsidiaries listed in Schedule 1, Bank Vorderland AG as agent and arranger, and the lenders listed in Schedule 2. Governing law: Swiss law; jurisdiction: Zurich.

Facilities: Term Facility CHF 100,000,000; Revolving Facility CHF 150,000,000. Final maturity: 28 November 2024.

## Clause 22 — Financial covenants
22.1 Leverage. The Borrower shall ensure that the ratio of Consolidated Net Debt to Consolidated EBITDA in respect of any Relevant Period shall not exceed 3.00:1.
22.2 Testing. Leverage is tested on each 30 June and 31 December by reference to the Compliance Certificate delivered within 90 days after the test date.

## Clause 23 — General undertakings
23.4 Distributions. The Borrower shall not declare or pay any dividend or other distribution unless, on a pro-forma basis, Leverage does not exceed 2.75:1 and no Default is continuing.
23.6 Loans out. No member of the Group shall make any loan to a person that is not a member of the Group, except loans in aggregate not exceeding CHF 15,000,000.

## Clause 26 — Events of Default
26.2 Financial covenants. Any requirement of Clause 22 is not satisfied. The Agent may, on the instructions of the Majority Lenders, cancel the commitments and declare all outstanding loans immediately due.
