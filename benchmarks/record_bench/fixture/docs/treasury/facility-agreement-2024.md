# Amended and Restated Syndicated Facilities Agreement (extract)

Amendment and restatement dated 27 June 2024 of the CHF 250,000,000 Syndicated Facilities Agreement dated 28 November 2019 ("2024 facility"). Parties as in the original agreement; Bank Vorderland AG as agent. Final maturity extended to 27 November 2027.

## Clause 22 — Financial covenants
22.1 Leverage. The ratio of Consolidated Net Debt to Consolidated EBITDA in respect of any Relevant Period shall not exceed 3.00:1.
22.2 Testing. Tested on each 30 June and 31 December; Compliance Certificate within 90 days after the test date, signed by the CFO.
22.3 Equity cure. Not available.

## Clause 23 — General undertakings
23.4 Distributions. No dividend or other distribution unless, on a pro-forma basis, Leverage does not exceed 2.50:1 and no Default is continuing.
23.6 Loans out. Loans to persons that are not members of the Group not to exceed CHF 15,000,000 in aggregate.
23.9 Refinancing. The Borrower shall present a refinancing plan to the Agent by 30 June 2027.

## Clause 26 — Events of Default
26.2 Financial covenants. Any requirement of Clause 22 is not satisfied.
